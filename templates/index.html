How the AI Resale & Rental Engine Works

A closed-loop platform bringing mathematical pricing transparency to the circular economy.

1. AI Valuation Engine

Our Scikit-learn Gradient Boosting model evaluates brand tier, material, age decay, usage frequency, and market transactions to calculate fair resale and daily rental prices.

2. Quality & Authenticity

Sellers provide condition disclosures and flaw mappings. Buyers inspect AI deal ratings before committing to either permanent purchase or event rental.

3. Flexible Rental & Booking

Need a tuxedo, evening gown, or trench coat for a weekend? Select rental dates, pay daily rates with protected security deposits, and enjoy zero closet clutter.

4. Seamless Return & Circularity

Use prepaid shipping labels to return rental garments. Deposits are refunded instantly upon inspection, and users earn Eco-Points for sustainable clothing reuse.
